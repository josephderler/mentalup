What Is the MentalUP Family Plan? How Does It Work?

MentalUP offers a Family Plan that allows you to create up to 3 profiles in one account. Thanks to this feature, you can have MentalUP’s benefits as a whole family.

How Do I Turn on the Family Plan Feature?

When you log in to MentalUP, you'll see a welcome screen asking, "Who is using MentalUP?” Click the "Add" button to create a new profile. That's it!

How Do I Switch From One User Profile to Another?

There are two easy ways to switch between user profiles in MentalUP:

  1. Select the profile you want to log into when you open the MentalUP app.
  2. If you don't want to restart the app, go to the Settings menu and select the profile you wish to use.

MentalUP Family Plan makes it easy to synchronize your data across multiple devices & works on all platforms. You can use it at the same time and have fun as a whole family!
