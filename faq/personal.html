Getting Started with MentalUP
What is MentalUP?
MentalUP is developed by child development experts, academicians, and professional game designers to be more than just a game! It supports brain and body development through educational games, live events, competitions, tests, and fun fitness workouts.

Millions of children worldwide use MentalUP to improve their cognitive skills, such as attention, memory, and problem-solving. In MentalUP, all contents are designed like fun games. They're engaging and always fun!
What age is appropriate for using MentalUP?
MentalUP has been carefully selected and developed by doctors, educational scientists and child development specialists. It is appropriate for all kids who are 4-year-olds and above. MentalUP is child-safe and completely ad-free.

Our platform is also used by millions of adults to enhance their memory, attention and concentration skills.

In MentalUP, all exercises begin at the easiest level. According to your achievements, AI of MentalUP will provide proper levels to improve your skills efficiently.
What is the MentalUP Family Plan? How does it work?
MentalUP offers a Family Plan that allows you and two children to train, track, and grow together! Thanks to this feature, you can benefit from MentalUP SUPER (premium) as a whole family.

MentalUP Family Plan can only be used by SUPER (premium) users. Free users cannot create or edit multiple accounts.

Learn more about How to Use a Family Plan in this article.
Does MentalUP have any other material or apps?
MentalUP is a subscription-based digital product. Therefore, you don’t need any other materials to use.

We recommend following our social media accounts. Our experienced curators always share good tips you may find useful. However, these materials are not related directly to using the MentalUP app.
How long should kids play mentalup games each day?
While MentalUP is a great tool to help children improve their cognitive and physical skills, we recommend that you set a daily limit on play time, as recommended by child development experts.

Every child's learning speed and habits are different. That's why MentalUP leaves it up to parents to restrict the use of the app.

Recommended use: Approximately 10 games per day and a quick workout for a few minutes.
Does MentalUP contain ads?
MentalUP is an education platform that has a pedagogical product certificate.

MentalUP isn’t an ad-supported app, or It doesn’t contain (including the freemium version) any ads to make sure that it keeps children safe.

It’s not just all about ads. MentalUP doesn’t contain any harmful or misleading objects in the apps.
Why are exercises too easy?
Exercises in MentalUP are shaped by the intelligence potential of the user, not the age of the user.. MentalUP benefits from AI and a complex algorithm while creating an exercise level for you. However, the AI needs to analyze your potential in the first few exercises. You can feel that the first levels are too easy, but workouts will be more consistent after a while.
Why isn’t MentalUP SUPER (Premium) Free?
MentalUP is not an ad-supported app. Like all "no ads" EdTech companies, we need financial resources to keep MentalUP updated, improve the overall experience, and continue to provide services.

Therefore, we use individual licensing methods such as subscriptions or in-app purchases. This way, we aim to protect children from all harmful content, including advertising.

Click here to learn more about the differences between the Freemium and SUPER versions of MentalUP.
Does MentalUP help dyslexia?
MentalUP can be used as a helpful resource to support the education of dyslexic children. It has many scientific educational games, including verbal brain teasers, word puzzles, attention exercises and memory games. In MentalUP, all the activities are designed like funny games. They’re engaging and always fun to play!
Does MentalUP improve students’ academic performance?
MentalUP makes it easier to learn new things and make practice thanks to it’s gamified nature. Students can maximize their cognitive skill potential and be successful in school by improving classroom and exam performances.

It has been scientifically proven that MentalUP improves attention and relevant cognitive skills capacity in regular use.

Account Help
How do I change my username or password?
  • To change your password, open the MentalUP app and then go to the Settings > Change Password menu.
  • To change your username (email address), open the MentalUP app and then go to the Settings > Contact Information menu.
Note: If you haven’t connected an email address to your account yet, you will use MentalUP as a guest user. To create an account, open the app and then go to the Settings > Create an Account menu. You can create an account and easily set a password from this menu.
I forgot my password. How do I reset my password?
If you forget your password, you can request to reset your password.
  • Go to the password reset page from the login screen.
  • Enter your email address and click the “Recover Password” button to send a password reset link to your email box.
  • Check the email address connected to your account for a password reset email.
  • From the email, click “Reset Password” and enter your new password.
  • Click Change Password to confirm your new password.
  • Now, you’re ready to log in to your MentalUP account from all devices using your new password.
My all progress is lost, and it started from scratch. Why?
It’s mostly because you’re logged in with your old or another account. Also, it is possible that you might have created a new account and logged in mistakenly.

First, please make sure you’ve logged in with the correct account, following the Settings > Contact Info menu. If you’re seeing that you’re using the wrong account, go to Settings and click Log Out.

Once you successfully logged out, click the “Already member? Sign in” button to log in to your own account.

Don’t remember your account email address or password? Click here..

How do I sign into my account from other devices?
If you created an account with your email address and password, you could sign in to your account from any device.

If you haven’t connected an email address to your account yet, you will use MentalUP as a guest user. To create an account, open the app and go to the Settings > Create an Account menu.
How do I change age, grade, and skills settings?
You can edit your profile information, such as name, year of birth, class, or skill selection, from the "My Profile" menu. To access it, click on your avatar icon at the top left on the home screen.
I can read/write and do math, but the exercises are too hard
We recommend that you disable exercises that require reading and math skills, at least until you have improved your skills enough.

To do this, go to the "My Profile" menu by clicking on your avatar icon at the top left of the home screen. Then click on "Birth Year", from this menu you can change your Math and Reading preferences.
I don’t want to receive MentalUP emails
If you don’t like to receive emails from us, you can change your opt-in by following one of these steps:
  1. Login to your account and go to the Settings > Contact Info menu. Then, unclick the “I want to be notified by email or notification about performance reports, campaigns, blog posts, and other topics” box and click the Save button.
  2. When you receive an email from MentalUP, click the “unsubscribe from this list” link from the bottom and approve your opt-in change to the following page.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Note: You must cancel your account through the same platform you used to begin your subscription.

For iOS and iPadOS - App Store:
  • Open the Settings app.
  • Tap your name.
  • Tap Subscriptions.
  • Tap the subscription that you want to manage.
  • Tap Cancel Subscription.
*If you don't see Cancel Subscription, the subscription is already canceled and will not be renewed. When you cancel a subscription, you can still use your subscription for the time you've already paid for.
Android - Google Play Store:
  • Open the Google Play Store.
  • Tap Menu > Subscriptions.
  • Select the subscription you want to cancel.
  • Tap Cancel subscription.
  • Follow the instructions.
*When you cancel a subscription, you’ll still be able to use your subscription for the time you’ve already paid.
Website (via mentalup.co.uk)
  • Log in to your account via MentalUP
  • Go to the Settings > Membership info > Cancel
