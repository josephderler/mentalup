About MentalUP

With over 15 million happy users worldwide, MentalUP is an acclaimed educational platform and winner of the "Best Family-Friendly Product" award.

It has been certified for pedagogical quality by the Education Alliance Finland. Also, MentalUP is a participant in the University College London Institute of Education EDUCATE Program.

Designed by child development experts, academics, and professional game designers, MentalUP is more than just a game! It helps children succeed in school and effectively improve their lifelong skills. MentalUP has no ads. It is a safe and beneficial playground for kids.

MentalUP has been widely used by children ages 4 to 13 to improve cognitive skills such as attention, concentration, focus, memory, and problem-solving. Besides, MentalUP is used by adults who want to give their brains new momentum.

All exercises in MentalUP are designed to be in game form. That's why kids don't see these exercises as a responsibility but as fun.

MentalUP is not just about brain games or exercises. There are many content types inside, such as educational games, learning games, IQ tests, live events, competitions, and even fitness workouts for their bodies.

How Can You Reference or Mention MentalUP?

MentalUP may be referenced as using its full name:

  • MentalUP - Educational Games for Kids

  • MentalUP - Games for Kids

Besides, we’re okay to see just “MentalUP” :)

How to Spell MentalUP?

MentalUP should be written as a string of letters. "M" and "UP" should be capitalized, but others are lowercase. If you want, you can write them all in upper case.

  • Correct Spelling: MentalUP
  • Alternative Spelling: MENTALUP
  • Examples of Misspelling: mentalup, Mentalup, mentalUP, mental up, Mental UP
